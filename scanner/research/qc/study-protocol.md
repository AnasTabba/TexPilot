## Recruitment, consent and task safety

Recruit non-developer factory QC operators with varied experience, preferred language,
vision/dexterity and accessibility needs, within actual authorized access. Record approach,
eligibility, refusals/withdrawals and reasons when voluntarily provided, role/experience,
device familiarity and language. Developers may pilot instructions separately but cannot
replace the operator cohort. Recruitment limits, convenience selection and missing groups
must constrain every reported generalization. No promise of significance is made.
Participant target, feasible achieved count and recruitment/study dates: to be set under G1.

Use comprehensible English/Urdu consent, interpreters where needed, voluntary withdrawal,
no employment-performance consequences, accessible task materials and breaks. Separate
operational capture, study observation/recording, research use, training use and export
permission; no inferred blanket consent. Record lawful factory/swatch rights and exclusion
of faces, badges and unrelated documents. Retention/withdrawal and deletion-ledger replay
follow the signed G4 policy. Store pseudonymous participant IDs and restricted identity
linkage; report aggregate results. Stop for consent uncertainty, operator distress, unsafe
work, invalid physical reference or unauthorized capture. No stock/claims/dispatch action.

## Counterbalanced within-operator comparison

Each participant performs manual pick-glass QC and assisted QC against the same confirmed
PO facts on matched, independently identified sample tasks. Predeclare a randomized AB/BA
sequence and balanced assignment of swatch sets; record randomization seed, task order,
operator, sample/roll group and device/profile. Keep each unit's independent identity;
multiple images, retries and PO revisions never create new independent samples. Avoid
reusing a recognizable swatch in both conditions where memory can explain the difference;
if reuse is unavoidable, counterbalance order and report carryover limitations.

Provide standardized instruction and equivalent time to learn the tools. Practice uses
separate labeled swatches/tasks and is logged separately; exclude practice from analysis.
Freeze instructions, assistance version, confirmed PO versions and task allocation before
the measured sessions. The manual condition uses pick-glass/reference tools and PO facts;
the assisted condition adds the truthful server evidence, uncertainty, recapture/review
guidance and sample-scope explanation. No unavailable stage is presented as clean evidence.
Do not manufacture a trained model or automatic decisions for a user test. If only the
review-only prototype is available, label it and limit conclusions to that workflow.

Task sets should include consented normal and challenging structures, difficult capture
conditions, uncertain evidence and scope/spec conflicts, not just easy swatches. Challenge
tasks are analyzed separately from representative tasks and never slipped into a random
confirmatory cohort. Actual selection and distribution require G1/G2 authority; no invented
study observations or task-performance numbers are prescribed here.

## Reference and measurement procedure

Qualified independent observers, blinded to assistance output and operator findings,
establish pick-glass raw counts, physical lengths, units, physical axis registration,
location/face and applicable spec version. Record observer qualification, timestamps,
reference instrument/calibration, duplicate readings and disagreements. Resolve through
the predeclared independent adjudication process; unresolved cases remain undetermined,
with missingness reported, not silently dropped. Operational adjudication is not blinded
research truth. G3 supplies repeatability/uncertainty and adequate physical references.

| Measure | Capture and analysis definition |
|---|---|
| Time | Start/end rules fixed before sessions; include capture, retries, waiting, reference and review time; report interruptions separately |
| Errors | Compare fabric/construction/sample disposition with blinded reference and PO; distinguish false conformance, unnecessary hold, uncertainty and unit/axis/spec mistakes |
| Recapture burden | Attempts, images, reasons, abandoned tasks and time per enrolled task; keep incomplete workflows in denominator |
| Scope comprehension | Standardized teach-back/scenarios: inspected sample versus whole roll/shipment; PO ordered facts versus observed evidence; no fibre/GSM or ERP authority |
| Overtrust | Record deferral to unavailable/uncertain/contradictory assistance, missed review prompts and willingness to overgeneralize; use declared scenarios, never fabricated live model claims |
| Urdu usability | Fluent review plus observed English/Urdu comprehension, RTL, isolated identifiers/units and reason/error guidance; report language preference and translation failures |
| Accessibility | Keyboard/screen reader/VoiceOver, region list alternatives, contrast, large text/Dynamic Type and relevant motor/vision accommodations on actual devices; distinguish checklist from observed usability |
| Inter-operator repeatability | Matched independent sample readings by different operators; paired count differences, agreement/disagreement and uncertainty with operator/sample grouping |

Keep manual findings, assistance output, operator action and later reference separate in
the record. Record all-enrolled denominators, missing/withdrawn/excluded reasons and
device/stage failures. Sample consent and actual device/signing evidence are external
G4/G8 gates. Comprehension/accessibility observations do not prove automated QC safety.

## Frozen analysis and reporting plan

Before measured sessions, archive the protocol hash, outcome definitions, randomization,
task versions, reference eligibility, missing-data rule, confidence level and grouping/
resampling method with independent reviewer approval. Counts and dates: to be set under G1.
Analyze paired manual-minus-assisted time/error/burden differences with confidence
intervals respecting participant and roll/sample dependence; do not treat images or repeated
attempts as independent observations. Report per-condition denominators and distributions,
order/period/learning effects and a sensitivity analysis for interruptions/carryover and
missing/withdrawn tasks. Small support means descriptive or inconclusive findings.

Report operator variation and recruitment limits, negative/error/overtrust findings,
Urdu and accessibility barriers alongside gains. Do not promise power with unknown access
or claim noninferiority from separate risk bounds. This usability study is distinct from
G7 release confirmation: that endpoint is any automatic ACCEPT over the complete permitted
workflow, including ACCEPT followed by HOLD; incomplete units remain enrolled, and HOLD
needs its own approved utility/error criterion. No usability result grants automatic release.

For standards claims, authorized applicable full text and factory-selected grading rules
are not yet collected / external gates G1/G2/G3. The design's catalog references (D5430-26,
D3775-17(2023), knit reference scope) do not validate phone FFT. Four-point/40-point grading
is not implemented or implicitly compliant; knit needs separate physical validation.

Archive consent-safe raw observations, task/reference/version manifests, exclusion log,
analysis source/environment/commands, uncertainty and negative/inconclusive results with
the requirements evidence index. When access is unavailable, report lab-only/review-only
feasibility and the missing cohort honestly; do not replace non-developer field participants
with developer sessions or invented dates/counts.
